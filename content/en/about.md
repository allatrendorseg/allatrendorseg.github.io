*People for Animals 2013 Foundation*

Our foundation is an organization of citizen volunteers that was founded in 2013.
Our founding members have several years of animal rescue experience.
For us, animal rescue is a vocation, and we do it voluntarily in our spare time.

## Our Foundation's Goals

- Rescuing injured, abused and endangered animals, and neutering cat colonies living on the streets.
- Veterinary treatment and temporary placement of rescued animals, providing their physical and mental rehabilitation, and their final adoption.
- Teaching children about correct and responsible pet ownership and animal protection laws through presentations.
