## REpont donation

You may have already come across REpont, the new bottle return system.

When returning bottles, you can choose to get the deposit back or donate it to charity.
If you scan a QR code that belongs to a unique ID, the amount goes to the bank account linked to it.

If you scan the Animal Police Foundation's QR code, our foundation receives the amount for the bottles, so you directly help the animals in our care.

## Corporate sponsorship

Become part of our mission!

If you would like to support the work of the Animal Police Foundation in the long term – with regular financial donations, in-kind donations or as a sponsor –, we will gladly accept your offer and, as far as we can, express our gratitude through a mutually beneficial partnership.

What can you expect from us as a key supporting partner?

For larger or ongoing contributions – depending on the type and amount of support – we offer a tailored partnership that can benefit both sides.

As part of our partnership, we offer our supporters the following benefits:

- **Tax benefits:** As the Foundation has public benefit status, we issue an official certificate for financial or in-kind support that can be used in your tax return.

- **Promotion opportunities:** By agreement, we feature our supporters in our publications, on our online platforms (website, social media), and at our events, on our posters and stands.

- **Custom appearances:** On request, we also offer creative, targeted appearances, for example in a jointly developed campaign or event.

With your support, you not only contribute to the development of animal protection, but also set an example for others, while your brand or business can appear as a socially responsible actor.

If you would like more information about support and sponsorship opportunities, please [contact](../contact.md) our team!

Thank you for also believing it is important to give a voice to those who cannot stand up for themselves!
